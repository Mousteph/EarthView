# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in EarthView welcoming and respectful. Contributors should be able to collaborate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Give and accept feedback professionally; focus discussion on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.
- Follow project guidance and moderator requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the [Mousteph GitHub profile](https://github.com/Mousteph). Do not use public issues for private reports.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, pull requests, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
